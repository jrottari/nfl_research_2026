# Data-source licensing

Most data consumed through nflverse is distributed under CC BY 4.0. Preserve
the nflverse attribution when publishing derived datasets, tables, or reports.

FTN charting data is different: it is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Any
published output incorporating Tier-4 `load_ftn_charting()` features must carry
FTN attribution and must be shared under compatible CC BY-SA terms. The code
does not silently remove or relabel FTN-derived columns.

Third-party rankings, contract, and projection sources may impose additional
terms. Confirm their current source licenses before redistributing raw data.
